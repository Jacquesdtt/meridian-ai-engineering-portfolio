A little context about this demo

The queue cut (0.70055) and rate limit (3.5%) follow the project. Cases 01 and 02 use approximate scores and account values from the two committed demo fixtures. Case 03 is invented. All cases are illustrative, not real customers or an evaluation set.

Scores rank monthly cases; they are not calibrated chances of leaving. Account signals are descriptive, not live SHAP explanations. Drafts use a local template, and the audit trail exists only for this tab. Reloading or choosing “Start fresh” clears it. The view switch illustrates permissions, not authentication.

The full application also includes model explanations, portfolio analysis, and report Q&A. Explore the full application guide ↗
